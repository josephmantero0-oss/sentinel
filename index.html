Frequently Asked Questions

Is my browsing data sent to external servers? +

No. 100% of all link analysis, blacklist checks, and YouTube content filtering happen locally inside your browser process using local JS service workers.

Will this slow down web browsing speed? +

No. The detection heuristics execute in less than 1 millisecond using Manifest V3 asynchronous non-blocking APIs.

How to whitelist a blocked website? +

You can bypass warning screens by clicking "Show Advanced" -> "Proceed Anyway (Whitelist)". The domain will be saved in local storage.
